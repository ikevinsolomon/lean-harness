Lean Harness

Drop the chat into any site.

This page is a stand-in for your own site. The button in the corner is the same assistant, in floating mode.

<script type="module" src="https://your-server/lean-chat.js"></script>
<lean-chat endpoint="https://your-server" mode="floating"></lean-chat>

Other modes: mode="inline" fills a box on your page; mode="page" is the full-screen app at /.
